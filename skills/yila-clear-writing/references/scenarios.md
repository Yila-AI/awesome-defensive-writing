# Scenario routing

Use the relevant section to preserve the conventions and information needs of each task.

## Research
Keep methods, population and evidential strength attached to findings. When polishing, separate language edits from unsupported factual concerns. When answering a reviewer, evaluate the specific objection and report actual changes only.

## Work and decisions
Lead with the decision or status, then the supporting facts, owner and next action when provided. Keep real dependencies. Convert vague risk lists into condition, consequence and response; do not invent a risk register.

## Public and product writing
Explain what a reader can do and under which verified conditions. Remove empty modesty without making universal performance or availability claims. Keep sponsorship disclosures and material exclusions.

## Teaching and interpersonal writing
Keep prerequisites, failure conditions and courtesy that serves the relationship. A valid apology should acknowledge what happened and the remedy; removing defensive filler must not erase responsibility.
