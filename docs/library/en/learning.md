## DW-055 · Learning advice

```text
Make the supplied material clear, direct and informative. Organize it around the
judgment or action the reader needs. Remove repetitive retreat and empty preambles.
Ground facts, numbers, scope, responsibility and commitments in the material; keep
meaningful conditions next to the claims they qualify. List missing facts
separately.

Task: Base actionable advice on starting level, goal and available time. Retain
path-changing prerequisites without stopping at 'it depends' or guaranteeing mastery
by a deadline.

Output: A learning path with starting level, next exercise and completion checks.

Input material:
Starting level: [fill in]
goal: [fill in]
available time: [fill in]
resources: [fill in]
```

<details>
<summary>See a related case · EX-19 · A learning record is not proficiency</summary>

**Facts**

Fictional learner: an undergraduate with two hours a week completed three introductory lessons and can read CSVs with an example, but cannot independently clean missing values. The near-term goal is independently cleaning a small table with missing values. There are no research projects or publications.

**Before**

I am just a poor beginner, although I have mastered data analysis. Learning varies by person, so there is little to say.

**After**

I have completed three introductory lessons and can read CSVs using an example. Next I will practice handling missing values independently, using my two hours a week to clean a small table and check whether I can repeat it without the example.

**Reason**

Use observable capability, a specific gap and a checkable task instead of self-denigration or fabricated mastery.

<summary>See a related case · EX-20 · Experience, resources and next steps</summary>

**Facts**

Fictional material: the author used exercise-based resource A for one table-cleaning exercise. Resource B consists of theory notes, with no completed exercise by the author. The reader wants table-cleaning practice with one hour weekly. Prices, update dates and general effectiveness are unknown.

**Before**

Please don't criticize me; this is only my personal view. A and B are both good and bad; A will definitely make anyone proficient in a week.

**After**

If the goal is table-cleaning practice, start with exercise-based A. I used it for one cleaning exercise; B focuses on theory notes. Use the next hour for one A exercise, then check whether you can reproduce it independently. Prices and update status still need verification.

**Reason**

Make a goal-based recommendation grounded in actual experience and retain unknowns.
