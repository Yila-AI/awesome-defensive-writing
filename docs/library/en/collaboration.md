## DW-025 · Progress follow-up

**Mode**: Draft

```text
Make the supplied material clear, direct and informative. Organize it around
the judgment or action the reader needs. Remove repetitive retreat and empty
preambles. Ground facts, numbers, scope, responsibility and commitments in
the material; keep meaningful conditions next to the claims they qualify.
List missing facts separately.

Task: Ask about a specific deliverable, agreed timing and needed reply. Preserve
courtesy, remove repeated apologies and do not turn a target date into a
prior commitment.

Output: A short follow-up naming the deliverable, actual agreement and requested
reply.

Input material:
Deliverable: [fill in]
Actual agreement: [fill in]
Requested information: [fill in]
```

<details>
<summary>See a related case · EX-25 · A progress follow-up with a clear reply time</summary>

**Facts**

Constructed collaboration: Chen agreed to deliver design figures Friday; today is Thursday. To schedule integration testing, the owner needs Chen to confirm progress and whether Friday delivery is on track by 17:00 today. There is no evidence of a missed deadline.

**Before**

Sorry to trouble you again; I know you may be busy. Perhaps you could look at the figures whenever convenient. No rush, and sorry for the inconvenience.

**After**

Chen, the design figures are due Friday as agreed. To schedule integration testing, please confirm current progress and whether Friday delivery is on track by 17:00 today. Thank you.

**Reason**

Keep normal courtesy while stating the existing delivery agreement and the reply needed now; remove the conflicting no-rush message.

<details>
<summary>See a related case · EX-09 · Decline scope expansion courteously</summary>

**Facts**

Fictional collaboration: this week's agreed deliverable is a Chinese abstract. An English full-text request is new and unagreed. The team has no capacity this week and can assess its schedule next week, without promising completion then. The Chinese abstract remains due Friday.

**Before**

We are terribly sorry for our limited capabilities and may not do well, but we will try to meet everything.

**After**

The Chinese abstract remains due Friday as agreed. We cannot add the English full text this week, but can assess its schedule next week. Please confirm whether you would like that assessment arranged.

**Reason**

Retain the existing obligation and set a truthful boundary for the added request without using false promises as courtesy.

<details>
<summary>See a related case · EX-10 · Ask honestly and retain responsibility</summary>

**Facts**

Fictional email: Lin is a third-year undergraduate with one data-cleaning course assignment and no papers. Lin wants to ask whether a supervisor could give brief advice on a data-quality topic. Lin missed a Tuesday commitment to send materials through an omission; the materials are now ready and attached.

**Before**

I am not competent enough to trouble you. The earlier materials may not have been sent on time for various reasons, and I have caused inconvenience. I may have some immature ideas about the topic; please advise.

**After**

Hello Professor, I am Lin, a third-year undergraduate who completed a data-cleaning course assignment. I would appreciate brief advice on a data-quality topic, if you are available. I apologize for omitting the materials I had agreed to send Tuesday; they are now attached.

**Reason**

Replace self-deprecation with actual identity and a specific request; acknowledge the omission and provide the remedy.
